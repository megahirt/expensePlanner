Private by design

Your finances never leave your computer

  • No account to create, no server, no analytics, no ads.
  • Bank and card data only enters through files you download and choose to open. Your bank password is never asked for.
  • Access to amazon.com is asked for only when you press Import, and you can take it back at any time.
  • One button in Settings deletes everything.
What it does

From a pile of statements to a clear picture

Sorts spending into categories on its own

It uses your own rules, what an Amazon order contained, a built-in list of well-known merchants and your bank's own category. Whatever it cannot place is grouped by merchant, biggest first, so one choice files every matching row.

Counts each purchase once

An Amazon order and the card charge that paid for it are one expense. They are matched side by side for you to confirm. Cancelled orders, returns and card payments are left out of your spending.

Tells you why

Every automatic category says how it was chosen. A category you set by hand is never overwritten, and every import can be undone.

Finds subscriptions and regular bills

Charges that come at a steady rhythm are listed with what they cost per year, next to spending by category and by merchant.

How it works

Three steps, no sign-up

1. Download your statements

Export a CSV from each bank and card, as you would for a spreadsheet.

2. Drop them in

The columns are worked out for you. Importing the same dates twice is safe: rows you already have are skipped.

3. Add Amazon, if you like

Amazon has no export, so the extension reads your order history page for you, with each item.

Not ready to use your own data? A made-up household's year of spending is built in. Switch to it in Settings to look around first. It is kept completely apart from your data.
